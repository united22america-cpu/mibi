Mibi is a calm self-reflection experience: one thoughtful question at a time, without pressure or a right answer.

What your results mean

There are no good or bad personality types. Mibi’s 40 questions are original, illustrative prompts and do not form a clinically validated psychological assessment. Results are a tool for self-reflection, not diagnosis or a prediction of your abilities.
