## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines personality discovery into an intimate, warm, and restorative digital sanctuary. Escaping the clinical, high-density, and monochromatic tropes of corporate SaaS assessments, the aesthetic centers around **Soft Organic Minimalism** mixed with warm lifestyle editorial sensibilities.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Gentle & Introspective:** Interactions feel unhurried, reassuring, and mentally decluttering. The visual rhythm encourages pause and reflection rather than rapid survey completion.
- **Empathetic & Warm:** Grounded deep slate copy combines with tender botanical and mineral pastels to establish safety, human warmth, and psychological comfort.
- **Tactile & Whimsical:** Generous 20–24px rounded cards, pill-shaped action triggers, soft sine wave line patterns, and geometric botanical motifs evoke physical mindfulness journals and artful wellness stationery.

### Visual Style Expression
- **Surfaces:** Dominated by Cloud Cream bases accented with expansive Soft Peach hero bays, Lilac Lavender discovery cards, and Sage Green growth accents.
- **Atmospheric Motifs:** Fine organic ribbon waves, subtle dotted ambient grids, and abstract botanical petals soften structural panels.
- **Polish Level:** High-craft Dribbble aesthetic with airy whitespace, subtle multi-stop ambient shadows, clean hairline boundaries, and pill-shaped iconography.

## Colors

The color palette shifts the personality assessment domain away from detached clinical blues and greys into a grounded, restorative natural spectrum.

### Core Palette Application
- **Cloud Cream (`#FAF8F5`):** Foundational canvas background. Soft and non-reflective, preventing visual strain during extended reflective reading.
- **Dark Slate (`#343B43`):** High-contrast anchor. Used for primary typography, dominant hero headers, stroke icons, and primary pill CTA buttons. It ensures WCAG AAA readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Soft Peach (`#F5DCD4`):** Primary hero container fill and warm highlight surface. Fosters immediate emotional welcome in banner sections and primary feature enclosures.
- **Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`):** Growth, balance, and natural calm. Applied to progression indicators, grounding trait scores, and affirmative test statuses.
- **Dream Lavender (`#DCD6EC`):** Intuitive, dreamy accent tone. Dedicated to introspection metrics, abstract badges, and personality category cards.
- **Muted Rose (`#D9A8A9`):** Gentle emotional highlight. Used for sentiment markers, feeling spectrum indicators, and organic wave flourishes.
- **Card Cream (`#FFFFFF`):** Elevated inner card containers and crisp input surfaces.
- **Subtle Border (`#EFEBE4`):** Hairline perimeter strokes for light cards, secondary button outlines, and progress tracks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes friendliness, legibility, and geometric balance using **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its rounded terminals and open apertures provide a clean, modern aesthetic that feels personal and accessible.

### Typographic Principles
- **Hero & Headline Presence:** Headings are set tight with subtle negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) and high contrast in Dark Slate (`#343B43`), communicating thoughtful authority without harshness.
- **Airy Body Text:** Continuous paragraphs and question descriptions use a relaxed line height of `1.6` to `1.65` to avoid visual density and support relaxed cognitive processing.
- **Eyebrow Tags & Metadata:** Section banners and micro-chips utilize `label-sm` with slight tracking (`+0.04em`) in uppercase or title-case to frame introspective milestones (e.g., "A LITTLE JOURNEY INWARD").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes expansive breathing room, centered compositions, and calm visual grouping.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width canvas bounded at `1160px` centered within Cloud Cream viewports. 12-column fluid grid for dashboard overview, transitioning to a focused single-column `680px` container for individual assessment questions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` outer margins. Insight cards and personality trait modules adapt into comfortable 2-column grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Floating personality preview badges wrap or stack smoothly below primary hero copy.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Space (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Links icon badges to trait labels and manages internal chip padding.
- **Macro Space (`space-lg`, `space-xl`):** Generates wide margins separating major reflective modules, keeping each personality pillar independent and distraction-free.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered warm surfaces, pastel backdrops, and low-opacity ambient drop shadows rather than sharp corporate elevations.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas:** Cloud Cream (`#FAF8F5`), completely flat and matte.
- **Tinted Tonal Bays (Hero & Feature Panels):** Soft Peach (`#F5DCD4`), Dream Lavender (`#DCD6EC`), and Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`). These surfaces rely on chromatic difference with zero shadow to delineate large functional zones.
- **Elevated White Containers:** Primary test answer panels, metric insight modules, and modal drawers sit on Card Cream (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate hairline border (`1px solid #EFEBE4`) and an ultra-soft wellness shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.04), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.02)`.
- **Interactive Hover Floating:** Interactive archetype tiles and pill buttons elevate subtly on hover:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.08), 0 6px 14px -3px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.03)` with a smooth `250ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` transition.

## Shapes

The geometric design language embraces rounded, organic contours that evoke softness, safety, and modern wellness products.

### Curvature Guidelines
- **Primary Hero & Card Bays:** 20px to 24px (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`), producing smooth, pebble-like card corners.
- **Interactive Badges & Personality Archetypes:** 16px to 20px (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) for compact floating elements (e.g., "The Dreamer", "The Explorer").
- **Buttons, Eyebrows & Form Controls:** Strictly **pill-shaped** (`border-radius: 9999px`) to reinforce tactile friendliness and eliminate sharp corners entirely.
- **Ornamental Accents:** Continuous sine-curve ribbons, dotted matrix fields, and petal-inspired botanical geometry embedded directly into panel corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background Dark Slate (`#343B43`), text Card Cream (`#FFFFFF`), full pill radius (`9999px`). Internal padding: `0.875rem 2.25rem`. Label set in `label-md`. Includes an optional trailing micro-arrow (`↗`). Hover slightly expands shadow with 200ms ease.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #343B43` or `#EFEBE4`, text Dark Slate (`#343B43`).
- **Pastel Floating Pill:** Subtle background tints (e.g. Sage Green or Soft Peach at 30% fill), text Dark Slate, zero border.

### Archetype & Personality Preview Cards
- Compact, pebble-shaped tiles (`width: 90px–110px`, `border-radius: 20px`).
- Colored with pastel washes: Dream Lavender (`#DCD6EC`), Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`), and Soft Peach (`#F5DCD4`).
- Centered clean line-art icon (spark, sprout, sun, or heart) in Dark Slate, paired with a small two-line archetype label in `label-sm`.

### Insight & Benefit Cards
- Constructed on Card Cream (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` border of `#EFEBE4` and `20px` corners.
- Includes a circular or organic pastel badge hosting a botanical or mindfulness glyph in the top left.
- Bold title in `title-md` followed by airy explanation copy in `body-sm`.

### Question Progression & Dimension Sliders
- **Track:** Smooth pill-shaped capsule (`height: 10px`, background: `#F0ECE6`).
- **Indicator Fill:** Segmented or dual-sided rounded fills in Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`) and Dream Lavender (`#DCD6EC`).
- **Thumb:** Circular white disc (`20px`) with a soft slate drop shadow.

### Selection Chips & Eyebrow Pills
- Pill-shaped tags (`border-radius: 9999px`) with `0.4rem 1rem` padding.
- Light pastel or semi-translucent backdrop (`rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.06)`), text Dark Slate (`#343B43`), styled in `label-sm` with tracking.
