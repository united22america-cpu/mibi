A little room to figure yourself out.

Some days you’re rereading a message from your ex. Other days even replying to a friend feels like a lot. We made Mibi for the moment you pause and ask, “What do I need today?”

Our story

While working with young people in Myanmar, we learned how much easier it is to ask a big question when someone makes time to listen. Mibi brings that idea to everyday life. A personality quiz or a quiet Mind Detox prompt gives you somewhere to begin.

Our team includes alumni of U.S. leadership programs. We’re planning a collaboration with a Tokyo-based Japanese psychology specialist and life coach with nine years of nonprofit experience.
