GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Is the personality test free?

Yes. You can take the personality assessment and explore your result without paying. More detailed guides may be introduced later.

Does Mibi diagnose mental health conditions?

No. Mibi is designed for personal curiosity and reflection. It is not a psychological diagnosis, therapy, or a replacement for professional support.

Is Ask Mibi already connected to live AI?

Not yet. Ask Mibi currently offers an interactive guided demo. Live AI functionality is being developed and is not advertised as available today.

Do I have to share private information?

No. Share only what you are comfortable with. Avoid entering sensitive personal or health details into the demo. Check the site’s privacy information before using future connected services.

Can I use Mibi in Indonesian?

Yes. The main experience supports Bahasa Indonesia, with other language options available while translations continue to improve.
