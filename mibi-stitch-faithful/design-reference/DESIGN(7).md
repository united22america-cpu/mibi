## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a gentle, self-reflective environment centered on personality growth, emotional equilibrium, and daily insight. Built upon a warm clay aesthetic, the visual atmosphere is tactile yet weightless, offering reassurance and lightness across every touchpoint.

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Tactile Modernism blended with Soft Organic Minimalism. Surfaces feel smooth, matte, and hand-molded rather than strictly computational.
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, serene, and playful without being childlike. The interface prioritizes spaciousness, curved silhouettes, and breathable negative space.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Introspective clarity, comforting discovery, and unhurried exploration.

## Colors

The color palette draws from earthen pigments, ceramic glazes, and sunlit mornings. The palette operates strictly in a light mode format to reinforce an open, airy feeling.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary Peach (`#E8AFA8`):** The signature brand accent. Reserved for focal interaction points, active progress highlights, and identity badges.
- **Secondary Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`):** Represents grounded reflection, emotional balance, stability metrics, and affirmative states.
- **Tertiary Dusty Lavender (`#DCD6EC`):** Signifies intuition, deep psychological archetypes, contemplative tags, and secondary categorization.
- **Neutral Deep Slate (`#343B43`):** High-readability dark slate applied to primary typographic levels, critical indicators, and high-emphasis controls. Never pure black.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas:** Cloud Cream (`#FAF8F5`) acts as the foundational wash. Warm White (`#FFFFFF`) forms distinct raised surfaces. Soft Peach (`#F5DCD4`) defines secondary visual containers and hero wash treatments.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Border Light (`#EAE5E0`) provides structural clarity without rigid demarcation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes human warmth, rounded forms, and open, unhurried reading cadence across all screen touchpoints.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines, Display & Brand Titles:** Set in **Nunito** (weights 600, 700, 800) for a rounded, approachable, and comforting tone.
- **Body & Continuous Reading:** Set in **DM Sans** at weight 400. Line heights are tuned generously (1.6x - 1.7x) to deliver an open, relaxed reading atmosphere that prevents cognitive fatigue during long reflections and assessments.
- **Labels & Interactive Controls:** Set in **Nunito** (weights 700, 800) to keep pills, tags, and small UI elements punchy, organic, and readable.
- **Zero Serifs:** All serif typestyles and serif system fallbacks are explicitly barred across both display and reading surfaces.

### Multi-Script & Localization Fallback Stack
To guarantee visual continuity and preserve friendly, non-serif geometric proportions globally, use language-appropriate sans-serif fallback chains:
- **Hindi (Devanagari):** `'Noto Sans Devanagari', 'Mukta', sans-serif`
- **Thai:** `'Noto Sans Thai', 'Kanit', sans-serif`
- **Burmese (Myanmar):** `'Noto Sans Myanmar', 'Pyidaungsu', sans-serif`
- **Vietnamese:** Covered natively by `Nunito` and `DM Sans` with extended Latin diacritics; fallback to `'Be Vietnam Pro', sans-serif`.
- **Cyrillic:** Covered natively by `Nunito` and `DM Sans`; fallback to `'Noto Sans', system-ui, sans-serif`.
- **Global Base Fallback:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements an adaptive fluid grid anchored by expansive, breathing margins that prevent cognitive fatigue during introspective tasks.

### Grid Systems
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid layout with `margin` (2rem, scaling to `margin-lg` 4rem on wide screens) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Max container width is 1200px for general dashboards, narrowing to a centered 720px single column for focused survey flows.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-card trait summaries compress into 2-column sets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (1.25rem) margins and `gutter-sm` (1rem) gutters. Floating utility controls adapt into fixed, bottom edge-padded bars.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component density remains intentionally airy. Closely related interactive items rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm`, structural card bodies leverage `space-md`, and major section transitions use `space-lg` and `space-xl` to establish clear breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is articulated through physical "soft clay" tactility—layered tonal planes accompanied by large-radius, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with warm base tones.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Cloud Cream (`#FAF8F5`) surface. Flat, static, unshadowed.
- **Level 1 (Default Card Plane):** Warm White (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces positioned above `#FAF8F5`. Elevated via subtle, warm ambient diffusion: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(232, 175, 168, 0.08)`. Outlined with a fine `1px solid #EAE5E0`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating States):** Applied to active survey choices, dragged cards, or floating utility controls: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(232, 175, 168, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom Sheets):** Reserved for contextual drawers and full-profile overlays: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.12)`.

### Border Philosophy
Avoid harsh contrast borders. When division is necessary, employ Border Light (`#EAE5E0`) at 1px thickness. Elevated cards utilize hairline borders to retain edge crispness against light backgrounds.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is organic, smooth, and friendly. Structural corners favor a pill-inspired curvature (Level 3), preventing visual friction.

- **Primary Cards & Panels:** Bound by 20px to 24px radii (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Controls:** Buttons, chips, search pills, and segmented toggles utilize full-radius rounded capsule geometries (9999px / pill).
- **Clay Metaphor:** Forms avoid sharp 90-degree points entirely, evoking soft-pressed ceramic stones or smooth river pebbles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background in Deep Slate (`#343B43`) with text in Warm White (`#FFFFFF`), or Primary Peach (`#E8AFA8`) with Deep Slate text for welcoming confirmation states. Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), height `3rem` to `3.5rem`, horizontal padding `1.75rem`. Typography set in Nunito weight 700.
- **Secondary Pill:** Background in Warm White (`#FFFFFF`), border `1px solid #EAE5E0`, text in Deep Slate (`#343B43`). On hover, background shifts softly to Cloud Cream (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** No border or surface fill. Text in Deep Slate with subtle hover state using 10% opacity tints of Soft Peach (`#F5DCD4`).

### Cards & Discovery Tiles
- **Structure:** Background in Warm White (`#FFFFFF`), 24px corner radius, hairline border in `#EAE5E0`.
- **Archetype Insight Card:** Inset with soft accent header zones using pastel fills (`#F5DCD4` or `#DCD6EC`) with soft organic boundary curves separating media from copy. Inner padding set to `1.5rem` to `2rem`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Insight Badges:** Pill-shaped, minimal height (`28px` to `32px`), padding `0.25rem 0.875rem`, typography in Nunito weight 700.
- **Tonal Variants:**
  - *Growth/Grounded:* Background `#B7CBB9` with 25% alpha, text Deep Slate (`#343B43`).
  - *Intuitive/Insight:* Background `#DCD6EC` with 35% alpha, text Deep Slate (`#343B43`).
  - *Personality Core:* Background `#F5DCD4`, text Deep Slate (`#343B43`).

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkbox)
- **Radio Buttons:** Outer circle `22px`, styled as a clay-pressed pill container. Unselected: `1.5px solid #EAE5E0`, background `#FFFFFF`. Selected: border and fill in Deep Slate (`#343B43`) housing an inner circle dot in Warm White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Checkboxes:** Squircle-shaped (`8px` border-radius), transitioning smoothly to Primary Peach (`#E8AFA8`) on activation with an off-white checkmark icon.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Fields:** Pill-shaped rounded containers (`rounded-full`) or soft boxes (`rounded-xl`). Background in `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #EAE5E0`, padding `0.875rem 1.25rem`. Text in `#343B43` set in DM Sans weight 400.
- **Focus State:** Border color shifts to Primary Peach (`#E8AFA8`) paired with an ambient glow ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(232, 175, 168, 0.25)`.

### Assessment Scales & Rating Pips
- Scaled horizontally from 1 to 7 or agreement matrix. Unselected states appear as soft cream pips (`#FAF8F5` bordered with `#EAE5E0`). Selected states morph dynamically into an expanded pill filled with Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`) or Primary Peach (`#E8AFA8`), conveying personal alignment without cognitive stress.
