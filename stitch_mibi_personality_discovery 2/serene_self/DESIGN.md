## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an introspective, serene, and restorative digital environment for self-discovery. Merging the contemplative tactility of a wellness journal with the refined typographics of an editorial publication and the crisp structure of a modern SaaS tool, the interface intentionally slows down interaction rhythm to alleviate assessment fatigue.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Contemplative & Unhurried:** Generous margins, airy leading, and balanced tone prevent visual overwhelm, instilling clarity during self-reflection.
- **Warmly Authoritative:** Grounded slate text paired with warm editorial display fonts confers credibility without the cold detachment typical of clinical psychology assessments.
- **Tactile & Organic:** Softly tinted containers and quiet outlines emulate textured, archival stationery.

### Design Style Expression
The visual aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Editorial Wellness** and **Soft Minimalism**. Surfaces are light, creamy, and gentle. Depth is achieved via tonal steps and delicate hairline dividers rather than aggressive drop shadows. Visual accents are introduced in low-saturation pastoral pastels (sage, dusty lavender, muted rose) to communicate cognitive domains without cognitive overload.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around grounded slate accents over restorative, warm, unbleached paper tones, accented by quiet botanical and mineral pastels.

### Primary Roles & Functional Mapping
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5` - Cloud Cream):** The foundational application backdrop. Never stark digital white; it anchors the warm, non-glare reading environment.
- **Primary Content & Structure (`#343B43` - Deep Slate):** Primary text, dominant headlines, focused icon strokes, and high-priority action buttons. Yields optimal readability against light tiers without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` - Warm White):** Interactive assessment modules, elevated outcome cards, and active form controls.
- **Accents & Personality Dimensions:**
  - **Growth & Grounding (`#B7CBB9` - Sage Green):** Progress indicators, positive confirmations, perception/sensing cognitive metrics.
  - **Intuition & Interiority (`#DCD6EC` - Dusty Lavender):** Type categories, cognitive function chips, introspection milestones.
  - **Affinity & Heart (`#D9A8A9` - Muted Rose):** Feeling/value spectrum markers, active emotional state indicators, gentle warnings.
  - **Atmosphere & Highlighting (`#F5DCD4` - Soft Peach & `#F8EEE2` - Pale Sand):** Hero banner fills, personality archetype wash panels, contextual callout modules.
- **Dividers & Sub-Text:**
  - **Border Line (`#EAE5E0` - Light Border):** Structural hairline dividers, pill borders, card perimeters.
  - **Secondary Copy (`#737A7B` - Muted Gray):** Subtitles, meta-tags, helper notes, and secondary telemetry data.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances expressive editorial serifs with a rational, human-centered sans-serif for multilingual clarity.

### Family Pairings & Roles
- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for archetype revelations, hero proclamations, section titles, and deep assessment prompts. Its refined stroke modulation evokes literary quarterly publications and journal covers. Use italic variants selectively for emphasis or poetic archetypes (e.g., *The Idealist*).
- **Interface & Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Drives all question options, quantitative personality trait sliders, navigation labels, interactive buttons, and long-form descriptive profiles. Offers consistent legibility across dense dashboard metrics and diverse linguistic alphabets without feeling rigid.

### Multilingual & Text Rhythm Rules
- Line height across continuous reading passages remains open (`1.6` to `1.7`) to give complex self-reflections visual room.
- Small labels and dimensional tags utilize subtle positive tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) with semibold weights to maintain silhouette definition against tinted pastel pill fills.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout adheres to an uncompressed, fluid grid designed around rhythmic cadence, ensuring focus is concentrated on the introspective discovery flow.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid. Max-width container at `1280px` for discovery dashboards; tightened to a centralized `768px` single-column layout during linear assessment flows to prevent scanning fatigue. Gutters fixed at `1.5rem`, outer margins at `3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout. Margin collapses to `2rem`, gutters at `1.25rem`. Diagnostic traits split into a 2x2 modular matrix.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins. Personality trait comparison bars and prompt sequences reflow to vertical stacks.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Internal Component Rhythm:** Driven by the `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) tokens for tight contextual affinity (such as an icon paired with an MBTI trait metric).
- **Structural Pacing:** Large jumps using `space-xl` (40px) demarcate distinct cognitive question groups and personality report chapters, reinforcing an unhurried, book-like pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects intense dropshadows in favor of surface tonal layering, soft hairline borders, and atmospheric ambient diffusion.

### Hierarchy Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground plane rendered in Cloud Cream (`#FAF8F5`). Flat and non-reflective.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Main dashboard cards, question panels, and trait summary boxes rendered in Warm White (`#FFFFFF`). Boundaries are defined cleanly by a `1px` stroke of Light Border (`#EAE5E0`), accompanied by an ultra-soft, low-opacity ambient haze: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.02)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating & Active Layers):** Dropdown menus, archetype modal drawers, and active assessment selections. Surfaces lift subtly using Warm White accompanied by: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(52, 59, 67, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 3 (Contextual Atmosphere):** Archetype highlight blocks and personality radar backgrounds leverage tinted washes (Soft Peach `#F5DCD4` or Pale Sand `#F8EEE2`) without borders or shadows, relying purely on chroma contrast to guide the eye.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects organic balance: curved contours that feel tactile, welcoming, and safe, avoiding sharp clinical angles.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Enclosed with `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) up to `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for expansive hero result containers. This curates a pebble-like silhouette.
- **Action Elements & Badges:** Primary interaction targets, toggle chips, cognitive tags, and text fields are strictly **pill-shaped** (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Inner Embedded Blocks:** Micro-elements inside larger cards (such as individual trait bar backgrounds or response slider tracks) adopt `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) to mirror the primary frame without causing nested visual dissonance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background Deep Slate (`#343B43`), text Warm White (`#FFFFFF`), full pill radius (`9999px`). Internal padding: `0.75rem 2rem`. Hover shifts to `#444C56` with smooth 200ms ease.
- **Secondary / Soft Pill:** Background Pale Sand (`#F8EEE2`) or Sage Green (`#B7CBB9` at 20% opacity), text Deep Slate (`#343B43`), border `1px solid transparent`.
- **Ghost Pill:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #EAE5E0`, text Deep Slate (`#343B43`). On hover, background shifts to Warm White (`#FFFFFF`).

### Chips & Personality Badges
- **Trait Tags:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`0.375rem 0.875rem` padding), using pastel tints tailored to trait groups:
  - Introversion/Intuition: Dusty Lavender (`#DCD6EC`) background, Deep Slate text.
  - Feeling/Perception: Soft Peach (`#F5DCD4`) or Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`) background, Deep Slate text.
- Text uses `label-sm` with semibold weighting to guarantee accessibility against pale surfaces.

### Question Response Selectors (Likert Scales)
- 5 or 7-point scale organized horizontally. Circles scale dynamically from subtle outer options to confident outer affirmations.
- Unselected states use Warm White fill with a `1.5px` border of `#EAE5E0`.
- Selected states fill with Deep Slate (`#343B43`) or Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`), pulsing a soft ambient ring outline.

### Input Fields & Text Areas
- Fully rounded (`9999px` for single-line inputs, `1rem` for open reflection fields).
- Background: Warm White (`#FFFFFF`), border: `1px solid #EAE5E0`. Placeholder copy: Muted Gray (`#737A7B`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to Deep Slate (`#343B43`) with a soft glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(183, 203, 185, 0.35)`).

### Cards & Result Summary Modules
- Built on Warm White (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` border of `#EAE5E0` and `rounded-xl` corners.
- Internal padding spans `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- Header features editorial headings (`Playfair Display`), paired with body narrative text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) at `1.6` line-height.

### Dimension Comparison Sliders (E vs I, S vs N, etc.)
- Track: Smooth capsule track (`height: 8px`, background: `#F8EEE2`).
- Fill: Left/right directional bars filled with Sage Green (`#B7CBB9`) and Dusty Lavender (`#DCD6EC`).
- Marker: Floating white circular thumb (`18px`) with subtle slate border indicating percentage tilt.
